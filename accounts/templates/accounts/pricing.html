Frequently asked questions
Can I switch from Free to Pro at any time?
Yes — upgrade whenever you're ready. Your existing links and persona settings carry over instantly. No re-setup needed.
What happens to my data if I cancel Pro?
Nothing is deleted. Your Brand Kit and extra links are preserved, but only one persona will be shown publicly until you re-upgrade. You can export your data anytime.
Is there a free trial for Pro?
The Free plan gives you a full feel for Prism with no time limit. Pro features are available the moment you upgrade — no trial period or hidden activation steps.
How does audience detection work?
Prism reads signals like referrer URL, device type, and time of day to intelligently serve the most relevant persona to each visitor — all without any extra setup from you.
